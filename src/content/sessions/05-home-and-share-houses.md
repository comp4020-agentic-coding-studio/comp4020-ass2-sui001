Every shared household runs on rules nobody voted on: who does the dishes,
whose food is communal, how loud is too loud after 11pm. They're rarely
written down because writing them down would mean admitting they're
negotiable — and most households would rather not have that conversation.

## Before the session

Identify one unwritten rule in your own living situation. Watch what
happens the next time someone breaks it: who says something, what they
say, and what changes afterward (if anything).

## In the session

We build a taxonomy of household rules — chores, noise, food, guests,
shared costs — and talk about which ones survive a change in the
household's membership and which ones quietly die.

## Afterwards

Keep this rule in mind for Week 11, when the course looks at how these
same rules shift as a household's relationships change or age.
