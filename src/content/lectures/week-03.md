## Outline

- Goffman's [interaction order](/readings/#interaction-order): the rules
  public space runs on, unstated and almost never taught directly
- [civil inattention](/readings/#civil-inattention) as the default courtesy
  between strangers, and [tie-signs](/readings/#tie-signs) as the signals
  that say "we're not strangers, don't extend it to us"
- the phone-while-walking case: attention as a resource you can withdraw
  from the room without anyone consenting to that
