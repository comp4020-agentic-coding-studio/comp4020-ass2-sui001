# Proxemics

How far apart people stand is a message, not an accident.

---

## Edward Hall's four zones

- **Intimate** (0–45cm) — reserved for the closest relationships, or forced
  by crowding
- **Personal** (45cm–1.2m) — friends, family, conversation at arm's length
- **Social** (1.2–3.6m) — colleagues, acquaintances, most daily business
- **Public** (3.6m+) — strangers, lecturers, anyone performing rather than
  conversing

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Which zone two people default to is rarely a conscious choice — that's
> exactly why it's worth watching.

---

## Reading a zone violation

- someone stepping into personal space uninvited is rarely random
- a forced intimate-zone crowd (a packed lift, a crowded train) suspends
  the normal rules, and everyone in it knows the suspension is temporary
- a relationship that outgrows its usual zone is a relationship changing,
  whether or not anyone's said so yet
