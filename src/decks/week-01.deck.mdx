# Coexistence 101

Before you can read a room, you need a working read on yourself.

---

## An old idea, in a lot of different clothes

Presence, emotional discipline, empathy and resilience aren't four
separate skills stacked on top of each other.

The first is what makes the other three possible.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> You can't consistently read what other people need if you're not
> tracking what's happening in you.

---

## Presence

Noticing your own attention before it's already gone somewhere else.

- what pulled your focus in the last hour, and did you choose it
- the gap between "I'm fine" and actually checking
- attention as the first resource this course asks you to manage

---

## Emotional discipline

Not suppression — the gap between feeling something and acting on it.

- naming the feeling buys you the gap
- the gap is where every good decision in this course happens
- Week 12 comes back to exactly this gap, under pressure

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## Empathy is a practice, not a trait

You don't "have" empathy the way you have a height.

You run it, deliberately, on purpose, in specific moments — and it gets
better with use, like anything else you practise.

---

## Resilience

The capacity to recover your baseline after a room knocks you off it.

- recovery is a skill you can watch yourself get better at
- Week 1's field task is the first data point on your own baseline
- everything after this week is you watching *other* people's
