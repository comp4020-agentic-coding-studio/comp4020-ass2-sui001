# Levels of resolution

You've learned to read the room. Now: what do you do when it's still
going wrong?

---

## The ladder

1. **Notice** — the friction is real, not imagined
2. **Name it privately** — to yourself, before anyone else
3. **Raise it directly** — with the person, plainly, without an audience
4. **Raise it with a third party** — when direct hasn't worked, or can't
   safely be tried
5. **Disengage** — when none of the above is working, and staying costs
   more than leaving

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> The lowest rung that would actually work is usually the right one.
> Most people skip straight to the top.

---

## Why people skip rungs

- rung 3 requires the emotional discipline Week 1 asked you to start
  building — without it, "raise it directly" turns into an ambush
- rung 5 feels safest but is also the most expensive: it ends the
  relationship it was supposed to repair
- the ladder only works in the moment if you already know your own
  baseline well enough to tell which rung you're on
